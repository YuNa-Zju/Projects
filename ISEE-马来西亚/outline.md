# 采访提纲

## 中文版

### 针对马来西亚科技部

1. 考虑到“一带一路”倡议下中国与马来西亚在科技领域的合作日益紧密，请问科技部未来在哪些信息与电子工程相关领域期待与中国企业或高校开展更深入的合作，以实现互利共赢？
2. 马来西亚政府如何鼓励年轻人对科学和技术产生兴趣？他们有没有一些面向学生的科技普及项目或者活动？
3. 马来西亚在发展科技产业时，面临的主要机遇和挑战是什么？科技部会如何应对这些挑战？
4. 对于我们这些外国学生来说，马来西亚的科技发展环境有哪些值得我们学习和体验的地方？科技部是否会支持一些国际交流项目？
5. 马来西亚科技部在推动国家科技发展方面扮演着怎样的角色？他们主要关注哪些领域，比如数字经济、绿色科技或者人工智能？

### 企业

1. 作为在马来西亚运营的科技企业，您认为 马来西亚在 **信息与电子工程领域**的营商环境有哪些优势和挑战？这些挑战是否阻碍了贵公司在本地的进一步投资或发展？
2. 针对马来西亚的消费者和市场特点，贵公司在产品研发、市场推广和本地化运营方面有哪些独特的策略？能否分享一些在马来西亚取得成功的案例？
3. 在“一带一路”倡议下，贵公司如何看待中国与马来西亚在信息与电子工程产业的合作前景？
4. 从技术和人才角度来看，贵公司在马来西亚是否有本地化的研发团队？未来在哪些信息与电子工程的细分领域，例如人工智能、大数据、云计算或智能制造，计划加大研发投入或寻求与本地高校的合作？
5. 贵公司在招聘人才时，除了专业知识，还会看重哪些能力？对于我们这些即将步入大学的工科学生，您有什么学习和成长方面的建议？
6. 未来几年，您认为信息与电子工程领域会有哪些重要的发展趋势？贵公司在这些趋势中将扮演怎样的角色？

### 校友会

1. 作为浙江大学在马来西亚的校友，您认为 浙江大学的信息与电子工程专业毕业生在异国的就业前景如何？哪些行业或领域对我们的专业技能需求较大？

2. 您在马来西亚的工作或创业经历中，是否感受到浙江大学的教育背景为您带来了独特的优势？在适应马来西亚的商业文化和社会环境方面，您有哪些经验可以分享？

3. 马来西亚校友会目前在促进校友职业发展、信息交流和校友间合作方面有哪些活动或平台？对于将来如果想向国外发展的同学，您有哪些建议和意见？

4. 作为浙江大学在马来西亚的校友，您认为在马来西亚学习和工作，与在中国有什么不同？有哪些是需要我们提前了解和适应的？
5. 您在马来西亚的工作或生活经历中，有没有遇到过一些挑战？您是如何克服的？这些经历对您个人成长有什么影响？

## Eng-version

### For the Malaysian Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation

1. Considering the increasingly close scientific and technological cooperation between China and Malaysia under the "Belt and Road" initiative, in which **information and electronic engineering-related fields** does MOSTI look forward to deeper collaboration with Chinese enterprises or universities for mutual benefit?
2. How does the Malaysian government encourage young people to develop an interest in science and technology? Do they have any **science popularization programs or activities** aimed at students?
3. What are the main **opportunities and challenges** Malaysia faces in developing its technology industry? How does MOSTI plan to address these challenges?
4. For us, as international students, what are the **valuable aspects of Malaysia's technological development environment** that we can learn from and experience? Does MOSTI support any international exchange programs?
5. What role does the Malaysian Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation play in **promoting national scientific and technological development**? Which areas do they primarily focus on, such as the digital economy, green technology, or artificial intelligence?

### For Companies

1. As a technology company operating in Malaysia, what do you think are the **advantages and challenges of the business environment in the information and electronic engineering sector** in Malaysia? Have these challenges hindered your company's further investment or development in the local market?
2. What are your company's unique strategies for **product research and development, market promotion, and localized operations** in response to the characteristics of Malaysian consumers and the market? Can you share some successful cases in Malaysia?
3. Under the "Belt and Road" initiative, how does your company view the **prospects for cooperation between China and Malaysia in the information and electronic engineering industry**?
4. From a **technical and talent perspective**, does your company have a localized R&D team in Malaysia? In which specific sub-fields of information and electronic engineering, such as artificial intelligence, big data, cloud computing, or smart manufacturing, do you plan to increase R&D investment or seek cooperation with local universities in the future?
5. When recruiting talent, besides professional knowledge, what other **abilities does your company value**? For us, as engineering students about to enter university, what advice do you have for our learning and growth?
6. In the coming years, what do you foresee as the **major development trends in the field of information and electronic engineering**? What role will your company play in these trends?

### For the Alumni Association

1. As a Zhejiang University alumnus in Malaysia, how do you view the **employment prospects for Zhejiang University graduates in information and electronic engineering** in a foreign country? Which industries or fields have a greater demand for our professional skills?
2. In your work or entrepreneurial experience in Malaysia, did you feel that your **Zhejiang University educational background** brought you unique advantages? What experiences can you share regarding adapting to Malaysia's business culture and social environment?
3. What **activities or platforms** does the Malaysian Alumni Association currently offer to promote alumni career development, information exchange, and collaboration among alumni? For students who plan to develop their careers abroad in the future, what advice and suggestions do you have?
4. As a Zhejiang University alumnus in Malaysia, what are the **differences between studying and working in Malaysia compared to China**? What should we know and adapt to in advance?
5. In your work or life experience in Malaysia, have you encountered any **challenges**? How did you overcome them? How have these experiences impacted your personal growth?
